Section 4: Present Simple (Hoy, Ahora)

Estructura Positiva:
Sujeto + am/is/are + Complemento (Lugar o Sentimiento)

El verbo To Be en presente se divide en tres formas. Analicemos juntos cuál le corresponde a cada pronombre:

AM

I am

I am happy.
(Yo soy / estoy)

IS (Singular)

He / She / It is

She is at school.
(Él/Ella/Eso es / está)

ARE (Plural)

You / We / They are

They are tired.
(Tú/Nosotros/Ellos son / están)

Section 5: Past Simple (Ayer, Antes)

Estructura Positiva (Pasado):
Sujeto + was/were + Complemento

¡En el pasado es más fácil! Solo hay dos formas: was para los singulares y were para los plurales y el pronombre 'You'.

WAS (Singulares)

I / He / She / It was

I was sick yesterday.
(Yo era / estaba)

WERE (Plurales)

You / We / They were

We were at the park.
(Nosotros éramos / estábamos)

Section 6: Negatives & Questions

Negatives (Añadir NOT)

Para decir "no soy" o "no estaba", solo agregamos not al verbo To Be. Normalmente lo contraemos (los juntamos).

  • is not ➔ isn't
  • are not ➔ aren't
  • was not ➔ wasn't
  • were not ➔ weren't
  • * Importante: "am not" NO se puede juntar como "amn't". Se contrae el sujeto: I'm not.

Questions (Cambio de orden)

En español usamos el símbolo "¿" al inicio. En inglés, movemos el Verbo To Be al principio de la oración.

Afirmación ➔ Pregunta

You are ready.
➔ Are you ready?

Afirmación ➔ Pregunta

He was at home.
➔ Was he at home?
