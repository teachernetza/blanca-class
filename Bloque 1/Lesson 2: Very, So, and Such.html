2. Grammar Fast-Recap

VERY

Significa "muy". Es el más común. Puede ir solo o con sustantivo.

  • It is very big.
  • A very big city.

SO

Significa "tan". OBLIGATORIO: El adjetivo debe estar solo.

  • The city is so big.
  • It is a so big city.

SUCH (A)

Significa "un ___ tan ___". OBLIGATORIO: Necesita un sustantivo al final.

  • Such a big city. (Singular)
  • Such nice people. (Plural)
